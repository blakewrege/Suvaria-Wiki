Fang Ro — Campaign Ledger¶

Player-facing campaign record. The statistics below are a legacy snapshot, not Fang's current level-12 sheet. They come from Fang Ro — Sheet ver7r11; the older Story ver7r3 is background material rather than a stat sheet.

Alignment: Neutral Good • Background: Outlander
AC: 17 • HP: 64 • Speed: 50 ft • Proficiency: +3

🛠 Legacy Class Features¶

  • Wolf Hybrid Form: Fang's transformation from the Mógu curse.
  • Wild Shape and Symbiotic Entity: Druidic transformations and fungal reinforcement.
  • Halo of Spores: 1d4 damage on the legacy sheet.
  • Agile Parry: +2 AC after making an unarmed attack while holding a kensei weapon.
  • Kensei Shot: Adds 1d4 damage to ranged weapon attacks for the turn.
  • Stunning Strike: Ki save DC 14.
  • Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, and Step of the Wind: Core ki techniques.
  • Slow Fall: Reduces falling damage by 25.
  • Feats: Magic Initiate (Warlock) and Mobile.
