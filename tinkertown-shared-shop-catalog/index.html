Francis’s Furniture¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

Nestled in a whimsically oversized train car, Francis’s Furniture is a rainbow jumble of couches, chairs, and cabinets—some plush, some peculiar, all hand-picked by Francis himself. A flamboyant gnome in a paisley vest, Francis prides himself on curating items “fit for the maddest hatters and the most sensible of halflings alike.” You’ll see chairs that float, tables that dance, and ottomans that purr. If you’re seeking the ordinary or the extraordinary, you’ve come to the right place!

Laughing Loveseat No actual magic here, but the upholstery is embroidered with smiling faces and bright motifs. Comfortably seats two small folk or one medium-sized visitor. 50 gp

Necessities and Nuances¶

Run by Nelly Nimbleneedle, a soft-spoken yet sharp-witted gnome with an eye for detail, Necessities and Nuances is the beating heart of Tinkertown’s daily life. Much like a classic mom-and-pop store, they stock everything from humble hygiene products to experimental potions—just the thing to keep a gnome’s gears running smoothly. Step inside, and you’ll see shelves stacked with everyday must-haves, plus a few extraordinary oddities that could only have been dreamed up in this whimsical, rolling town.

SoSo Mart¶

The SoSo Mart chain has spread across the land faster than a flock of griffons on half-price wing night—promising “savings, or so-so they say.” Every location looks the same: a huge warehouse-like interior with bright, garish signage insisting that everything is “Cheaper Here!” Run by the perpetually bland Manager Manny Monotone, this branch in Tinkertown towers over its modest neighbors, complete with a mechanical sliding door that greets you with a tinny “Welcome to SoSo Mart.” They advertise a 20% discount for members, but the membership fees are steep, and the fine print is very fine.

Advertising Slogan:
“You could pay more elsewhere… but that would be so so silly, wouldn’t it?”

Happy shopping (or so-so shopping)! Remember, at SoSo Mart:

“If it ain’t cheap, we’ll make it cheaper… eventually.”

 

Full Metal Racket¶

Run by Sergeant Ironbeard, a gruff dwarf veteran who paces around the shop barking orders at imaginary recruits, Full Metal Racket is Tinkertown’s one-stop depot for all things armament. Clad in a dented breastplate etched with slogans like “Gnome Mercy,” he firmly believes the town is “always one subterranean ambush away” from total disaster. Though he scoffs at “puny gnome gear,” he grudgingly stocks smaller-sized weapons and armor, too. Step into his cramped train-car workshop, and expect an earful of tough love—and maybe some top-notch arms if you can handle the rant.

The Wonderarium¶

Formerly Fredrick Fizzlebang’s Fireworks, this bright and bustling bazaar is owned by the one and only Fredrick Fizzlebang—a gnome with a spark in his eye and a perpetual wisp of smoke drifting from his rumpled top hat. While Fredrick’s passion for pyrotechnics remains, the rebranded Wonderarium now features all manner of trinkets, oddities, and potions that dazzle (and sometimes backfire) in classic gnomish fashion. Step inside for an explosion of colors, tinkling bell sounds, and the faint smell of singed hair. There is a small platform out front for fireworks demonstrations. Watch from a safe distance… or not.

Munster Mart¶

Once a humble stall hawking a few wedges of cheese, Munster Mart has grown into Tinkertown’s favorite fromagerie, famed for its robust dairy selection and Marbury Munster’s flamboyant mustache. The shop smells of toasted cheese, fresh bread, and the faint whiff of brine from cured meats. Impossibly tall shelves (for a gnome, at least) showcase artisanal cheeses, whimsical charcuterie boards, and, for the daring, a few “enchanted edibles.” Lately, Marbury Munster has been eyeing his new rival—Kardarsha, a male Kajiit traveling merchant (and boat-wagon entrepreneur), backed by Ella’s recent investment. Will Munster Mart maintain its cheesy crown, or will Kardarsha’s curiosities lure customers away?

Deep Rock Galactic Corporation¶

A battered storefront welded onto a rumbling train car, Deep Rock Galactic Corporation is the tough-as-stone mining hub of Tinkertown. Run by the no-nonsense dwarf Karl, the shop greets you with its rallying cry: “Rock and Stone!” A dusty dwarf helmet is perched on a rusty hook outside, and the interior is lit by flickering lanterns strung from the rafters. Miners of all races pass through, lured by rugged gear, arcane digging gadgets, and Karl’s famed one-liners (“If it ain’t dwarven, it ain’t worth dirt!”). Gnomes flock here too, bringing their flair for engineering to the grit of underground expeditions.

Store Highlights¶

  • Karl’s War Stories: He’ll often regale customers with tales of “the Drop Pod crash of ’39” or “that time a swarm nearly shaved me beard!”
  • Rock and Stone: The shop motto, yelled every time you buy something important. It’s said to bring good luck in the mines.
  • Gnome vs. Dwarf Rivalry: Gnome-made devices are sometimes more advanced, but dwarven gear is rugged and reliable. Karl tosses friendly jabs at “gnome tinkering.”

Whether you’re a pickaxe-wielding dwarf or a gadget-happy gnome, Deep Rock Galactic Corporation stands ready to equip you for spelunking, monster-bashing, and treasure-hunting. Remember: Rock and Stone!

 

Brews and Blues¶

Tucked away in a cozy corner of Tinkertown’s rolling streets, Brews and Blues is a dimly lit pub run by the mischievous twin gnomes Artie and Bartie. Nearly identical in height, voice, and even their smug grins. Their bar’s motto is: “When the tinkering stops, the drinking starts.”

Downstairs, patrons gather for hearty bar food, to sip creative cocktails, and to lose themselves in the gentle hum of gnomish blues tunes. Upstairs lies the Rumor Mill, a literal milling machine used in the brewing process (for crushing specialty grains), which churns so loudly that private conversations become whispers in the din. Perfect for rumor-seekers and rumor-makers alike.

The Rumor Mill¶

Up the squeaky stairs is the Rumor Mill—an actual milling contraption used for refining specialty grains in small experimental brew batches. The grinding noise provides perfect cover for hushed conversations. Here, you can glean or spread rumors. Some tidbits float freely among the barflies; others cost a little coin or require a bit of persuasion.

Quick Notes¶

  • Artie & Bartie: Identical twins except for one difference. They’ll gladly serve “Blue Bummer” to calm you down if Tinkertown’s hustle gets too intense.
  • The Rumor Mill: Pay a coin, roll a Persuasion check, or buy a drink or two—chances are you’ll glean some juicy secrets in the noisy upstairs.
  • Performers: Brews and Blues often features traveling bards, occasionally playing gnomish blues ballads about mechanical heartbreak and stolen steam-powered hearts.

Enjoy the ambiance, nurse a drink (maybe not too many “Blue Bummers”), and maybe walk away with some new intel—if the milling machine doesn’t drown out your eavesdropping first
