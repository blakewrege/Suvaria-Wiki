Suvaria Player Portal¶

The player-facing home for Suvaria: current handouts, character records, equipment, campaign rules, places, and everything the party has been legally permitted to remember.

No GM material lives here. Pages appear only after they are explicitly marked published.

Current Adventure¶

  • Session 078 handouts — the currently indexed session and surrounding adventure.
  • Browse handouts by session — current releases and the growing pre-077 archive.

Characters, Equipment & Rules¶

  • Player records — character ledgers, known history, major purchases, and important equipment.
  • Equipment & abilities — the complete master index for owned items, standalone cards, tattoos, relics, and player-safe abilities.
  • Rules & character options — table rules, reward spells, specializations, curses, and legacy mechanics.

Campaign History¶

  • Public Session Progress record — the long-form campaign timeline.
  • Pre-077 handout archive — older released material as it is audited and added.

Major locations continue to receive full wiki pages on Obsidian Portal. This portal handles the smaller, faster-moving material that should not require a new PDF every time someone buys a cursed stick.
