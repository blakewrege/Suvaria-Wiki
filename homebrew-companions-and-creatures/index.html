Time to Drop Knowledge¶

The blink dog is an intelligent canine that takes its name from its ability to blink in and out of existence, a talent it uses to aid its attacks and to avoid harm. Blink dogs harbor a long-standing hatred for displacer beasts and attack them on sight. Culture: Blink dogs tend to live in closely knit packs consisting of related individuals. Much like wolves, these packs are generally comprised of a family unit--a mother, a father, and their children. Unlike wolves, it is not uncommon for a pack to also include extended family members (grandparents, aunts, uncles) and the adult children of the pack, which are welcome to stay and expand the pack by bringing home a mate. Language: Blink dogs have their own language, a mixture of barks, yaps, whines, and growls that can transmit complex information. Combat: Blink dogs hunt in packs, teleporting in a seemingly random fashion until they surround their prey, allowing some of them to take advantage of flanking. Maturation Rate: Blink dogs will mature into an adult at about 10 months(at which point ask me for a new sheet). Warranted Suspicion: Blink dogs tend to be highly suspicious of other races, as the wicked and unscrupulous have long sought their fortune by stealing blink dog pups with the intention of "taming" and "training" them and then selling them for outrageous amounts of coin. Given that blink dogs are sapient creatures the equal of any human, dwarf, or elf, this practice is understandably viewed by blink dogs as kidnapping and slavery. Curious Companions: Although most blink dogs keep strictly to their own kind, some eccentric individuals chose to attach themselves to roving "packs" of adventurers, spurred on by curiosity.

18 (+4) 9 (-1) 16 (+3) 18 (+4) 14 (+2) 10 (+0)
___
- Condition Immunities charmed
- Skills Math +10, Investigation +7, Persuasion +3
- Senses darkvision 60ft., passive Perception 12
- Languages Orc, Common
___
Onion Stench. Any creatures within 5 feet of Ned develops an irrational craving for onion rings.

The Darnedest Thing. When you would be killed or reduced to 0 hit points you can choose to completely ignore the effect. If you choose to do so, tell a story of how you norrowly avoided death. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest.

A Bit Clumsy. Whenever Ned makes a melee weapon attack a random creature within 5ft must roll a Dexterity Saving Throw(DC 14) to avoid being knocked prone.

Calculating the Odds. Before a party member uses a skill you can ask the DM what the Diffuculty of the action is.

Actions¶

Accounting Ledger. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5ft., one target. Hit 6 (1d4 + 4) Bludgeoning Damage

Throw Money at the Problem. Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 30ft., one target. Hit Target is distracted by the shower of coins, next attack on the target has Advantage.

Beguiling Mathematical Equation (Encounter Power). Choose a creature with an Intelligence Score above 3 that you can see within 30ft. The target must succeed on a Intelligence Saving Throw(DC 15) or be Stunned with confusion for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make another Intelligence Saving Throw. On a success, the target snaps out of it. On a failure the target takes 2d6 Psychic Damage as it starts to develop a headache. Ned cannot perform other actions as while using this ability.

Think of the Insurance Costs! (Encounter Power, Reaction). You recite the Fundamental Accounting Equation(Assets = Liabilities + Owners Equity) explaining to your attacker that hitting you really isn't in their best interests. For the remainder of the encounter or until you attack it the target is Charmed by you. Only works on Humanoids.

Wilburt was just a normal pig until he subjected to terrible experimentation by Gandomire. The party found him caged in a frost giant's fortress. Ella freed him and now he follows her around like a lost puppy. His past is shrouded in mystery, if only someone in the party could speak pig...
