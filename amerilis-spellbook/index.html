🎓 Amerilis’s Spellbook – Level 12 Illusionist Elf Wizard¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

📜 Daily Spell Preparation¶

🎯 You may check up to 17 total spells per long rest (Wizard Level 12 + INT 20).
Cantrips are always known and don’t count toward the limit.

🪄 Cantrips (Always prepared — auto-scaled for level 12)¶

  • [x] Minor Illusion (Illusion Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 30 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action ⏳ Duration: 1 minute (Concentration not required)
    🔮 Create a sound or image that fits in a 5-ft cube. The image is static and silent; the sound can range from a whisper to a scream.
    🎭 Used to distract, disguise, or create false cover. Perfect for misdirection or creative problem solving. Creatures may investigate (INT check) to disbelieve.

  • [x] Prestidigitation (Transmutation Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 10 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action ⏳ Duration: Up to 1 hour
    ✨ Create harmless sensory effects. Excellent for flair, subtle signals, or RP flavor. Such as: flavor food, clean or soil an object, light/snuff small flame, chill/warm an item.

  • [x] Dancing Lights (Evocation Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 120 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action ⏳ Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
    💡 Create 4 torch-sized lights (dim light in 10 ft each) that float, move, or form into a glowing humanoid shape.
    🌀 Lights can move up to 60 ft as a bonus action.

  • [x] Ray of Frost (Evocation Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 60 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action
    ❄️ Fire a beam of cold at one creature:

  • Hit: 3d8 cold damage (Level 11+)

  • Effect: Target’s speed is reduced by 10 ft until your next turn.

  • [x] Fire Bolt (Evocation Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 120 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action
    🔥 Launch a mote of fire at a creature or flammable object:

  • Hit: 3d10 fire damage (Level 11+)

  • Special: Ignites flammable objects not worn or carried.

  • [x] Mage Hand (Conjuration Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 30 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action ⏳ Duration: 1 minute
    🤲 Summon a spectral hand that can: Open doors, pick up or drop items, interact with simple objects.

  • [x] Message (Transmutation Cantrip)
    📏 Range: 120 ft 🕒 Casting Time: 1 action ⏳ Duration: 1 round
    💬 Whisper a short message to a creature within range, even through objects or walls (as long as there's a path).

  • Target can whisper back immediately. Ideal for stealth missions, split parties, or hidden talk mid-combat.

🔹 1st-Level Spells - ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐¶

  • [_] Burning Hands — Action, Self (15-ft cone), DEX save. 3d6 fire; +1d6/slot.
  • [_] Chromatic Orb — Action, 90 ft, spell attack. 3d8 chosen type (fire/cold fits theme); +1d8/slot.
  • [_] Ice Knife — Action, 60 ft. Hit: 1d10 piercing (attack); then burst: 2d6 cold in 5 ft (DEX half); +1d6 cold/slot.
  • [_] Thunderwave — Action, Self (15-ft cube), CON save. 2d8 thunder; push 10 ft; +1d8/slot.
  • [_] Ray of Sickness — Action, 60 ft, spell attack. 2d8 poison; CON save or poisoned until end of your next turn; +1d8/slot.
  • [_] Sleep (Illusion-ish glamor) — Action, 90 ft. Affects 5d8 HP (no damage).
  • [_] Silent Image (Illusion) — Action, 60 ft, Conc., 10 min. 15-ft cube moving image (no damage).
  • [_] Disguise Self (Illusion) — Action, Self, 1 hr. Change appearance (no damage).
  • [_] Mage Armor — Action, touch, 8 hr. AC = 13 + Dex (no damage).
  • [_] Shield — Reaction, Self. +5 AC until your next turn (no damage).

  • [_] Scorching Ray — Action, 120 ft, spell attacks. Create 3 rays; each ray 2d6 fire on hit; +1 ray/slot.
  • [_] Flaming Sphere — Action, 60 ft, Conc., 1 min. Ram/end-of-turn 2d6 fire (DEX half); +1d6/slot.
  • [_] Dragon’s Breath — Action, touch, Conc., 1 min. Target gains 15-ft cone for 3d6 (fire/cold) as bonus action (DEX half); +1d6/slot.
  • [_] Aganazzar’s Scorcher — Action, 30-ft×5-ft line, DEX save. 3d8 fire; +1d8/slot.
  • [_] Snilloc’s Snowball Swarm — Action, 90 ft, 5-ft radius, DEX save. 3d6 cold; +1d6/slot.
  • [_] Mirror Image (Illusion) — Action, Self, 1 min. Three duplicates (no attack roll against you often).
  • [_] Blur — Action, Self, Conc., 1 min. Attacks vs you at disadvantage.
  • [_] Invisibility (Illusion) — Action, touch, Conc., 1 hr. Turn a creature invisible.
  • [_] Phantasmal Force (Illusion) — Action, 60 ft, Conc., 1 min, INT save. Mental illusion; 1d6 psychic/round if it “interacts.”
  • [_] Darkness — Action, 60 ft, 15-ft radius, 10 min. Magical darkness / area denial.

🔶 3rd-Level Spells - ☐ ☐ ☐¶

  • [_] Fireball — Action, 150 ft, 20-ft radius, DEX save. 8d6 fire; +1d6/slot.
  • [_] Melf’s Minute Meteors — Action, Self, Conc., 10 min. Six meteors; when cast and as bonus actions send 1–2 meteors (each 2d6 fire, DEX half). +2 meteors/slot.
  • [_] Sleet Storm — Action, 150 ft, 40-ft radius, Conc., 1 min. Difficult terrain, obscured; drops concentration (no damage on cast).
  • [_] Major Image (Illusion) — Action, 120 ft, Conc., 10 min. Large illusion incl. sound, light, smell.
  • [_] Hypnotic Pattern (Illusion) — Action, 120 ft, 30-ft cube, WIS save. Incapacitates/charmed on fail (no damage).
  • [_] Counterspell — Reaction, 60 ft. Interrupt a spell (no damage).

🟦 4th-Level Spells - ☐ ☐ ☐¶

  • [_] Fire Shield — Action, Self, 10 min. Choose warm (resist cold) or chill (resist fire); a melee attacker within 5 ft takes 2d8 (fire or cold, matching shield).
  • [_] Wall of Fire — Action, 120 ft, Conc., 1 min. 60-ft line or ring; one side deals 5d8 fire/round (DEX half); +1d8/slot.
  • [_] Ice Storm — Action, 300 ft, 20-ft radius, DEX save. 2d8 bludgeoning + 4d6 cold; difficult terrain; +1d6 cold/slot.
  • [_] Conjure Minor Elementals — Action, Self, Conc., 10 min. Elemental spirits swirl in 15-ft emanation; your attacks deal +2d8 elemental (acid/cold/fire/lightning) on hits in the area; +1d8/slot; enemies in area have difficult terrain.
  • [_] Summon Elemental — Action, 90 ft, Conc., 1 hr. Call a single elemental spirit (choose fire/air/earth/water); it fights for you with level-scaled stats.
  • [_] Greater Invisibility (Illusion) — Action, touch, Conc., 1 min. Invisibility that persists through attacking/casting.
  • [_] Hallucinatory Terrain (Illusion) — 10 min, 300 ft, 24 hr. Disguise terrain over a large area (no damage).
  • [_] Phantasmal Killer (Illusion) — Action, 120 ft, Conc., 1 min, WIS save. Frightened; 4d10 psychic/round on fails.

🟪 5th-Level Spells - ☐ ☐¶

  • [_] Cone of Cold — Action, Self (60-ft cone), CON save. 8d8 cold; +1d8/slot.
  • [_] Immolation — Action, 90 ft, DEX save. 8d6 fire (half on save); ongoing 4d6 fire at end of target’s turns (save ends).
  • [_] Wall of Force — Action, 120 ft, Conc., 10 min. Nearly indestructible barrier (no damage).
  • [_] Mislead (Illusion) — Action, Self, Conc., 1 hr. You turn invisible and create an illusory double.
  • [_] Conjure Elemental — Action, 90 ft, Conc., 1 hr. Conjure a Large elemental spirit (choose fire/air/earth/water); 2024 version uses an aura/space interaction to punish foes that enter/linger near it.

🟥 6th-Level Spells - ☐¶

  • [_] Wall of Ice — Action, 120 ft, Conc., 10 min. Create panels/dome. On appearance, creatures it cuts through take 10d6 cold (DEX half). Crossing the frigid sheet later: 5d6 cold (CON half). Upcast: +2d6 (on create) & +1d6 (on pass) per slot above 6th. ([Roll20][7])
  • [_] Otiluke’s Freezing Sphere — Action, 300 ft, 60-ft radius, CON save. 10d6 cold; special interactions with water; +1d6/slot.
  • [_] Investiture of Flame — Action, Self, Conc., 10 min. Fire aura + ranged line option; resist fire; attackers take fire dmg (values per spell).
  • [_] Investiture of Ice — Action, Self, Conc., 10 min. Cold aura + difficult terrain + ranged hail attack; resist cold.
  • [_] Programmed Illusion (Illusion) — Action, 120 ft, until dispelled. Complex illusion triggers on conditions (no damage).
  • [_] Disintegrate — Action, 60 ft, DEX save (negates). On fail 10d6 + 40 force; reduces to dust on 0 HP.

Default 17 (e.g., Fireball, Cone of Cold, Scorching Ray, Flaming Sphere, Dragon’s Breath, Wall of Fire, Fire Shield, Ice Storm, Wall of Ice, Sleet Storm, Hypnotic Pattern, Major Image, Greater Invisibility, Phantasmal Killer, Summon Elemental, Counterspell, Shield)
