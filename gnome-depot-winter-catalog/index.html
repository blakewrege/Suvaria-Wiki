🧰 Gnome Depot — Winter Catalog (Revised & Printable)¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

“If we can’t fix it, it didn’t come with instructions.” Location: Tinkertown Rail-Car Aisle 7 (look for the orange cog). Seasonal Special: 10% off non-magical tools with your Skyhook Scouts badge. Clearance Caveat: Cursed items are sold as-is. All sales final. Buyer be aware, wearer beware.

🛒 Store Policies¶

  • Returns: 30 days for mundane goods with receipt and intact tag.
  • Cursed Items: No returns, no exorcisms. We’ll loan you tongs.
  • Attunement Locker: Free, warded test-booth for 10 minutes (waiver required).
  • Bulk Order Desk: Build crews & field kits priced on request.
