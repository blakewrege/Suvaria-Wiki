🌸 Amerilis Lovelace Liadon – The Child of Undone Seasons¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

❄️ Racial Abilities¶

  • Darkvision. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.
  • Fey Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can’t put you to sleep.
  • Keen Senses. You have proficiency in the Perception skill.
  • Trance. Elves don’t need to sleep. Instead, they meditate deeply for 4 hours a day.

Frostveins¶

You have resistance to cold damage due to your Winter Eladrin heritage.

Igni Blood¶

Your Summer Eladrin heritage fuels your flames with ancestral wrath.
Once per turn, when you deal fire damage with a spell or ability, you can choose one instance of fire damage to gain extra damage.

The bonus fire damage is based on your character level: +1d4 / +1d6 / +1d8 / +1d12 at 1st / 5th / 11th / 17th level

🔥 The Mark of Thaurë’Vanya¶

(“Flame-Fated Grace” in Elvish-inspired etymology)
A forbidden union. A flame sealed in frost. A future not written, but burned into being.

World Lore¶

Among Feywilds's elven cultures, mingling Winter and Summer Eladrin bloodlines is not only taboo—it is feared. Each lineage embodies a seasonal ideal, and combining them is thought to disrupt magic, weather, and prophecy. Children born of both are said to be impossible.

But Amerilis was born with the Mark of Thaurë’Vanya, a mythic glyph long suppressed in song and legend. Upon her Eladrin awakening, it ignited—flame and feylight.

Prophecy fragments refer to her as: - “The herald of undone seasons” - “The flame that blooms in the snow” - “The child born of ice and fire”

Some fear her. Some seek her. Few understand her.

Mark of Thaurë’Vanya (Racial Feature)¶

You gain sorcery points equal to 1/3 your level (rounded up). These points can only be used to enhance or modify fire or cold spells.

  • These sorcery points refresh after a long rest.
  • If you are a sorcerer, treat this as a separate sorcery pool, usable only for spells that deal fire/cold damage. (Including elementals)

Suggested Metamagic Use:¶

  • Subtle Spell (1 SP): Remove verbal and somatic components—ideal for silent casting or spellcasting while restrained.
  • Distant Spell (1 SP): When you cast a fire/cold spell that has a range of 5 feet or greater, you can spend 1 sorcery point to double the range of the spell.
  • Empowered Spell (1 SP): Reroll damage dice for fire/cold spells (up to your INT modifier).
  • Careful Spell (1 SP): Prevent friendly fire; up to INT modifier allies automatically succeed on their saving throws.
  • Twinned Spell (1+ SP): Duplicate single-target fire/cold spells (e.g. Ray of Frost, Fire Bolt, Ice Knife).
  • Quickened Spell (2 SP): Cast a fire or cold spell as a bonus action.
  • Heightened Spel (3 SP): Give one target of the spell disadvantage on its first saving throw made against a fire/cold spell.

Cloak of Winter’s Grace¶

Wondrous item, Uncommen (requires attunement)

While wearing this ancestral garment:

  • +1 AC
  • +1 on all saving throws
  • You ignore cold-based environmental effects including difficult terrain

Your icy lineage pulses with subtle defiance, marking you as a scion of the Hoarfrost Reach.

“This was your grandmother Elvarelle’s,” Ivanna says. “Not as flashy as that flame-spitting gremlin, but we can’t have you running off without representing both sides of the family.”


🔮 Lindarcalen – The Emberdream Brooch¶

Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement by an elf or fey-touched creature)

“In the twilight halls of House Val’Ignira, where hearts burned with pride and dreams sang with flame, the brooch Lindarcalen shimmered with memory and power…”

A delicate brooch of rose-gold and veined moonstone, shaped like a flame entwined with a harp. It glows faintly warm, humming with a song only dreamers hear.

✨ Properties¶

Dreamfire Vision (1/long rest)¶

Enter a lucid Feywild dream after a long rest. Receive a symbolic vision (like legend lore) about: - Fey beings - Magical places - Forgotten histories

Dreams are poetic, eerie, and metaphor-laced.

Fey-Tuned Heart¶

You have advantage on Charisma checks with fey creatures and elven nobility. The brooch marks you as a blood heir of the Summer Court.

Mindfire Resistance¶

Advantage on saving throws vs. charm, fright, and magical control (e.g., dominate person, suggestion). Your mind is warded by the dreaming flame.

🔥 Flamelit Companion – Cinderkin¶

A tiny fey flame spirit bound to Lindarcalen. Mischievous, squeaky-voiced, and flickers like Calcifer from Howl’s Moving Castle.

Summoning Cinderkin¶

  • Bonus action to summon.
  • Floats on your shoulder or cloak.
  • Understands and speaks Sylvan.
  • Lasts 1 hour/day, extended by feeding wood.

Combat Effect – Emberflare Wings (1/combat, Reaction)¶

  • +5 AC until your next turn.
  • Hover 30 ft. in any direction (no opportunity attacks, as majestic flaming wings burst from your back). If you end your turn in the air you slowly glide to the ground.
  • Upon activation, a burst of dreamflame erupts in a 20-foot radius around you:
    • Each enemy in the area takes 4d6 fire damage.
    • Must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = your spell save DC) or be blinded until the end of their next turn, dazzled by the incandescent blaze of fey-born fire.

      “It was not the heat that stunned them—it was the light.
      Like watching the moment of their own unraveling.”

Utility Uses¶

  • Acts as light source or magical heater.
  • Animates small flames (e.g. teapots, fireplaces).
  • May whisper cryptic Feywild truths to Amerilis.

“They named you calamity before you spoke your name.
Not because you would end the world…
…but because you might choose not to follow its ending.”
