Welcome to Armskirk!¶

Pick From the Visitor Brochure Rack¶

Complimentary maps! Questionably complimentary advice! Take whatever catches your eye. Armskirk accepts no responsibility for missed trains, overconfident tour guides, regrettable haircuts requested by the customer, or anything a talking bird repeats under oath.

Looking Presentable, Feeling Better¶

Brochure What It Promises
Cloud Mirror Salon — Adventure, Apology, or Court Appearance? Haircuts, hot-towel shaves, beard shaping, braids, fur brushing, feather oiling, horn polishing, and emergency repairs after helmets. Ask for Farrah Faucet. She already knows why you are here.
Lotus Hot Springs Day Pass Madam Yu Zhen offers affordable public mineral baths, steam rooms, towels, scrubs, massage, laundry service, and neighborhood gossip at no additional charge.
Jade Steam Private Onsen Reserved mineral baths, carved screens, flowering courtyards, tea service, and staff professionally trained not to notice the next room. All guests must leave before sunset; overnight accommodations and eight-armed employees are officially unavailable.
Hundred Herbs Hall — Travel-Weary Remedies Affordable salves, combat tonics, monster oils, throwing powders, hangover remedies, and expert advice against drinking all of them at once.
Ink Dragon Tattoo House Consultation Zhen “Calamity” Jian offers protective tattoos, family crests, spirit animals, moonlit ink, and questions suspiciously better than the answers.
Silver Needle Tailors — Repairs While You Wait Travel-cloak relining, formal fittings, festival clothes, armor-friendly alterations, and new sleeves after the old ones met heroism.
Saint Dacius Civic Hospital Charity Evening Healers, donors, speeches, light refreshments, and opportunities to look generous in public while supporting one of Leonia’s finest hospitals.
Dr. Molar’s Miracle Mandibles Demonstration A live dental-strength exhibition promising teeth “strong enough to chew iron, soft enough for soup.” Dentists have requested we stop printing that sentence.

Xiaolong: Food, Shopping, and Culture¶

Brochure What It Promises
Xiaolong: The Little Dragon District A lantern-lit walking route through tea houses, noodle shops, import markets, shrines, bathhouses, salons, and street food stalls.
Xiaolong Night Market Tasting Pass Steamed buns, mooncakes, spicy skewers, rare teas, and at least one food item the brochure refuses to translate.
Snake Blood Alley Delicacy Crawl Monster-part cuisine, chili oil, exotic broths, waiver forms in very small print, and a commemorative spoon for survivors.
Golden Carp Tea House Ceremony Clementine Pekoe serves delicate teas, mooncakes, canal views, chess tables, and the usual order of customers who insist they have never visited. The old men are definitely not information brokers.
Moon Gate Open-Air Theater Tonight: The Last Lantern at Eclipse Ford, a classic Mian wartime romance featuring seven farewells, one sunrise, Lincohn with an axe, and no refunds for crying.
Thousand Feather Garden Aviary Jewel-plumed cranes, talking parrots, moon owls, thunderpheasants, and strict warnings not to repeat anything the birds say in court.
White Crane Dojo — Visitor Lessons Master Shrew Fu teaches crane forms, tiger forms, flowing-river forms, and the beginner discipline of discovering how flexible you are not. Visitors may observe quietly or ache educationally.
Little Dragon Shrine — Luck for the Road Incense, ribbons, tiny carved dragons, and a quiet place to ask for protection, honor an ancestor, or improve the odds of a risky journey or worse business deal.
Dragon Child Toy Shop Madam Lian Zhu and her grandson Bo offer carved dragons, wind-up lions, puzzle boxes, painted soldiers, paper lantern kits, and tiny clockwork beasts for children and suspiciously enthusiastic adults.
Nine Lantern Import Hall Silk, porcelain, spices, paper charms, enchanted fans, rare teas, medicinal roots, and questionable “authentic relics.”
Red Wheel Cartwright Demonstration Yard Lu Han, better known as “Cullen Bowhandle,” offers chariots, wagons, wheels, harnesses, axle repair, and a live demonstration of “proper load balance.” Fewer idiots loading it are not covered by warranty.

Food Worth Missing a Train For¶

Brochure What It Promises
Borb’s Buns — Hot Steam, Full Hearts Borb and Marda Bunbury serve bao, dumplings, meat pies, sweets, and a line full of dockhands, rail workers, market aunties, and customers who brought their own chairs. Order loudly if certain, bravely if not, and do not ask Borb for ketchup.
Kardarsha’s Curds & Curios — Arena Pop-Up Everyday wheels, battle bites, enchanted wedges, cursed clearance cheese, and samples sold by a cat who believes victory must be lined with dairy. Lactose-tolerance potions available for the brave but biologically unprepared.
Rainy Day Armskirk for Miserable Tourists Covered markets, tea houses, museums, bathhouses, indoor food stalls, and warm places to complain about the weather.

See the Respectable City¶

Brochure What It Promises
Grand Promenade Highlights A polished walking route through Armskirk’s finest hotels, hospitals, theaters, monuments, salons, and civic plazas.
The Lion & Crown Historic Hotel Tour Famous guests, private balconies, chandelier-lit halls, and old portraits that may judge you.
Old Armskirk in Two Hours or Less Alleys, monuments, old bridges, civic landmarks, and “authentic” history at a pace achievable only if being chased.
Museum of Heroic Misunderstandings Wax figures, battle dioramas, inaccurate plaques, and a gift shop selling tiny swords to children.
Museum of Illusions & Questionable Optics Impossible staircases, talking mirrors, vanishing doors, fake ghosts, and one hallway that insists it is longer on the inside.
The Grand Marionette Hall Royal scandals, tragic clowns, tiny guillotines, heroic mice, and family entertainment according to an unusually broad definition of “family.”
Armskirk Clocktower Ascent Sweeping city views, bell history, civic architecture, and mild regret halfway up the stairs.
Grand Library Researcher’s Pass Marble halls, spiral stacks, maps, newspapers, and assistance from Grand Librarian Horace Folio—provided your question is specific and your page corners remain unfolded.

Romance, Nightlife, and Plausible Deniability¶

Brochure What It Promises
Moonlit Canal Gondolas Private rides through lantern-lit waterways with music, wine, and dramatic sighing available for an additional fee.
Wetwood's Brokeback Outfitters — Get High Together! Clint Wetwood guides mountain excursions for bachelors, confirmed bachelors, and men who become confirmed somewhere above the tree line. Includes sturdy horses, matching chaps, sunset ram-watching, secluded cabins, the famous One Bedroll Discount, and family cave packages at Putty Nutty Caverns. Bareback is a personal decision; the insurance company requires saddles.
The Pink Pony Inn — Where the Fur Flies Madam Maeve Mane welcomes every well-behaved identity, while Butch Glassidy serves bardcore cocktails with professionally maintained plausible deniability. Add bar snacks, suspiciously modern lute remixes, dramatic performers, and enough private booths for everyone to mind their own business. No biting without consent.
The Gilded Ring Casino & Social Club Cards, dice, fine drinks, lavish rooms, and the reliable opportunity to discover which of your instincts is most expensive. Membership has privileges; losing is open to everyone.
The Velvet Cage — Tonight on Stage Cabaret, acrobatics, music, elaborate stage shows, and exclusive seating for patrons who prefer their entertainment lavish and their cloakroom questions minimal.
Lovers’ Lookout Picnic Basket Deal Bread, fruit, cheese, two cups, and one bottle of Regretful Red for a romantic outing above the city. Ravens not included but unavoidable.
Masquerade Ball This Weekend Masks, dancing, secret admirers, plausible deniability, and a complimentary ribbon no one explains.
Lust Not Included: Discreet Lodgings Guide Private rooms, nightlife, cabarets, companionship services, and “cultural experiences” with very careful wording.
Emergency Date Night Rescue Package Flowers, carriage, dinner reservation, apology card, and a backup excuse written by a professional liar.

Civic Business and the Trials¶

Brochure What It Promises
Official Trials Spectator Schedule Debate times, arena exhibitions, public ceremonies, approved chants, prohibited horns, and concession locations. Schedule accuracy not guaranteed after anyone says “surprise round.”
Hall of Accord Exterior Tour Statues, speeches, civic history, and absolutely no access to restricted doors no matter how confidently you walk toward them.
Iron Ledger Exchange — Visitor Appointments Currency exchange, letters of credit, contract escrow, appraisals, business loans, and financial advice from people who can detect arithmetic fear. Ask for Sterling Worth.

Beyond the Paved Streets¶

Brochure What It Promises
Putty Nutty Caverns — A Brokeback Family Adventure Come spelunk through the soft clay corridors of Armskirk’s most family-friendly natural wonder! Follow expert guides through glittering mineral pockets, echo chambers, and natural slides to earn the coveted “I Got Putty Nutty!” completion ribbon! Souvenir helmets are available in the gift shop. Putty Nutty Caverns has absolutely no connection to the thoroughly discredited rumor that a visitor died there. The tour company won the court case, and courts are famously never wrong.
Beginner’s Crystal Cave Excursion Glowing crystals, echo pools, cave moss, gentle paths, and rocks that cost extra if you lick them.
Advanced Spelunking: The Probably Safe Route Rope descents, tight passages, underground rivers, and the phrase “sign here first.”
The Friendly Bat Conservatory Cave bats, fruit bats, messenger bats, educational talks, and one bat the guide insists is “just large for his age.”
