Republic of Leonia¶

  • Type: Nation
  • Government: Federal democracy
  • Founding cities: Armskirk, Torath, and Corvos
  • Province: Mian Lung
  • Current capital: Armskirk
  • Head of government: Ringleader Lincohn Abraham
  • National symbol: The lion

The Republic of Leonia lies in the far east of Suvaria. It is the youngest nation on the continent, although Leonian officials have already produced enough speeches, statues, and commemorative mugs to make it feel much older.

Leonia did not exist before the alliance of Armskirk, Torath, and Corvos. About two centuries ago, the three sovereign free cities created a mutual-protection pact. That alliance gradually grew into shared laws, trade agreements, public institutions, and—after what historians politely call several energetic disagreements—the federal Republic of Leonia.

The founding cities were fiercely independent and had no interest in trading three local rulers for one distant monarch. The republic was therefore built to preserve local identity while giving the cities a common defense and national government. Leonian politics has been arguing over where local freedom ends and federal authority begins ever since. Most citizens consider the arguing an essential public service.

The Three Free Cities and Mian Lung¶

Each city keeps its own customs and civic identity while participating in the government of the republic.

  • Armskirk sits on the northwestern coast of Leonia's eastern peninsula. It is the current capital: a rain-polished city of government halls, markets, theaters, rail traffic, and confident public opinions.
  • Torath is a mountain city of rail yards, arenas, performance halls, strong cheese, and the serene conviction that everyone else is pronouncing it wrong.
  • Corvos is a coastal city of shipyards, lighthouses, aqueducts, and patient negotiators who consider silence a complete counteroffer.
  • Mian Lung is an older eastern realm that remained separate when Leonia was founded. It later entered a mutual-defense treaty with the republic and became a full province roughly one hundred years ago while retaining a Chancellor and many local institutions.

The national capital rotates among the three founding cities every twenty years. Mian Lung participates in the republic but is not part of the capital rotation. Armskirk currently has the privilege, responsibility, and municipal headache of hosting it.

Government¶

Leonian politics borrows its titles from the circus. This is not because government is a joke. It is because Leonians believe power should perform in public, face the crowd, and occasionally be pelted with fruit.

  • The Ringleader is the republic's elected head of government. A Ringleader serves one five-year term and cannot immediately stand for another.
  • The Barker Circle is the executive council. Its members administer the republic and explain its decisions to the public—sometimes in that order.
  • The Jugglers oversee taxes, tariffs, budgets, and trade. They insist the name refers to balancing competing needs, not making public money disappear.
  • The Stagehands oversee roads, rail, civic construction, and other public works. When everything functions, nobody sees them. When it does not, everybody suddenly becomes an engineer.
  • The Audience is the citizen body from which the government draws its legitimacy. Citizens vote, petition, attend debates, cheer, heckle, and maintain the sacred democratic right to complain about the results.

The Hall of Accord in Armskirk currently houses the national government. Its public chambers resemble an amphitheater more than a throne room: circular stages, packed galleries, speaking horns, banners, and enough acoustics to make a minor procedural objection sound like a declaration of war.

The Chancellor occupies Mian Lung's separate Jade Seat, not a seventh place in the Barker Circle. The seat carries a binding vote when national decisions alter the province's levies, military deployments, treaty rights, or the Jade-Lion Concord; otherwise the Chancellor advises the government with all the authority of a very old treaty and an extremely heavy binder.

Ringleader Abraham has reached his term limit and is stepping down rather than seeking a way around it. In Leonia, voluntarily leaving office is considered either the highest form of civic virtue or proof that someone has finally read all the paperwork.

The Trials of the Three Cities¶

Every five years, Leonia chooses its next Ringleader through the Trials of the Three Cities. The Trials combine ritual competition, public spectacle, political campaigning, and civic judgment. Teams put forward a candidate for Ringleader and compete before the Audience.

The exact sequence, scoring, and oldest rites are intentionally secretive. Some challenges test strength or skill; others test judgment, persuasion, teamwork, or the ability to remain dignified while the entire republic watches. Officials reveal rules when tradition requires it, which is a ceremonial way of saying at the last possible moment.

The current Trials opened in Armskirk with a spectacular Super Clash Bros competition. The surviving teams now advance toward a public debate. Beyond that, contestants receive the same reassuring guidance given to generations before them: prepare for anything, trust the judges, and sign the waiver.

The War of Broken Chains¶

Twenty-five years ago, neighboring Mycoterra was still the Confederated Cap States: thirteen slaveholding states ruled from Richmire by vampire aristocrats. Their laws called slavery Royal Bond Service and sold feeding rights alongside labor contracts. This improved neither the slavery nor the vampires, although it produced considerably more paperwork.

Enslaved Mycoterrans and abolitionists built their own liberation movement. Harriet Tubers guided families through the Underground Pipeway, Frederick Sporeglass rallied the country through speeches and newspapers, Ulysses S. Grantoad commanded the Army of the Free Caps, and William Tecumseh Shrooman broke Confederate supply lines during the March to the Green Sea.

Young Leonian Lincohn Abraham joined the rebellion as a vampire hunter, wielding his silver-edged axe The Railsplitter against officers who treated death as a brief scheduling conflict. Mian Lung's Jade Lantern Relief Company carried healers and engineers into the war, and its soldiers' water drums famously summoned Deuce Leem, guardian of Xuán Ji, at Eclipse Ford. The fighting passed through Fort Shroomter, Gettyspore, the Thwomp Trenches, and Eclipse Ford before Operation Breaking Dawn brought down Richmire and Blood Regent Toadstool.

The new government abolished slavery, established citizenship, protected voting rights, and rebuilt the country as the Sporeign Dominion of Mycoterra. Mycoterrans remain quick to remind foreign admirers that they liberated themselves; Lincohn helped with the unusually persistent vampires.

Mycoterra and the Open Ring¶

The neighboring Sporeign Dominion of Mycoterra spent twenty years rebuilding after overthrowing the slaveholding vampire government that once ruled it. Five years ago, Mycoterra petitioned to become another Leonian province. Ringleader Abraham vetoed admission under the charter's Free Assent Clause after auditors found suspiciously uniform votes, matching council resolutions, and communities that inspectors could not interview privately. Critics call the veto an old soldier's prejudice; supporters call unanimous politics a contradiction wearing a very cheerful hat.

This year's Open Ring Accord is the compromise. Mycoterra may sponsor the Redcap Resolutes for one Trials cycle, led by Marshal Marioni—a Mycoterran reformer who earned Leonian citizenship while rebuilding the Corvos floodworks. If Marioni wins, he may become Ringleader, but Mycoterra must still face a separate admission vote. Leonia has therefore addressed a major foreign-policy dispute in its traditional manner: public arguments, elaborate rules, and controlled property damage.

Mian Lung and the Eastern Connection¶

Mian Lung is Fang Ro's homeland, an eastern coastal province whose history predates the republic. Its merchants, craftspeople, immigrants, and soldiers have shaped Leonia for generations. Mian soldiers form the backbone of Leonian armed forces, while trade and migration have made their traditions part of daily life in the Free Cities.

Armskirk's Xiaolong District, or “Little Dragon,” is the most visible expression of that relationship. Founded generations ago by people from Mian Lung, it remains a center for lantern festivals, tea houses, martial schools, bathhouses, artisans, imported goods, and food worth missing a train for.

Fang Ro served among Mian Lung's warriors before traveling with the party. General Wei Shen and Black Chao also hail from Mian Lung, and their reputations carry considerable weight among soldiers and veterans in Leonia.

Mian Lung is presently facing a wave of fires and disappearances blamed on the Jǐn Bào, or “Golden Leopards,” whose leader Master Zhaoyan preaches purity through flame. Fang Ro has returned home to confront the crisis while Black Chao travels with the party. The Golden Leopards' ultimate purpose remains unknown.

Recent History¶

Before the alliance, Armskirk was an overseas crown port of Great Bickerland, the United Fiefdoms of Great Bickerland, ruled by House Toodor. Roughly 220 years ago, its rebels aided republicans in the homeland, with support from independent Torath and Corvos. The civil war ended with King George the Taxing's trial and execution in Great Bickerland—hence His Former Heightness. Armskirk became free and Great Bickerland became the Untied Fiefdoms, a separate republican commonwealth; neither restored the monarchy. Armskirk took the dismantled Ivory Tower home as reparations, then spent twenty years governing itself before the three free cities founded Leonia. Its delegates brought strong views about hereditary power and a rather pointed definition of term limits. Armskirk's royal history survives in its imported museum, armor shops, and sixty queens' worth of theatrical grievances. Great Bickerland is not a Leonian province; the war's allies were not already one nation.

The original Leonian pact was forged by Armskirk, Torath, and Corvos about two hundred years ago. Mian Lung was not a founding member. After the republic already existed, the independent Jade Kingdom signed a separate mutual-defense treaty with Leonia and remained a sovereign treaty partner for generations.

Roughly one hundred years ago, the Jade-Lion Concord made Mian Lung a full province of Leonia. The Concord preserved the office of Chancellor and a place for Mian Lung in national government while formalizing military, tax, and food obligations. The fact that everyone still argues about which side gave up more is considered proof of a balanced treaty.

The Lion Line, a recent joint rail venture with Tinkertown and EARS, has strengthened trade and travel between the republic's cities without turning Leonia into “the railroad nation”—a distinction its tourism office makes with surprising frequency.

The capital rotates Corvos → Torath → Armskirk, then repeats; each move loses at least one ministry and several good chairs. Famous former Ringleaders include Louie the Fourteenth-Time, who built fourteen fountains and one working sewer; Vicky Regina, who wore black and doubled the national tea budget; and Otto von Biscuit, who unified the tariff code through pastries and threats.
