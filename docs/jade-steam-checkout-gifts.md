# Jade Steam — Checkout Gifts and Night Wages

*Session 078 · End-of-shift reward selection*

![Yu-Banya reluctantly presents all ten enchanted checkout gifts on a lanternlit bathhouse counter.](https://i.imgur.com/1juWZQn.png)

> “Your service has been recognized. Management is exploring its legal options.” — Madam Yu-Banya

Return your borrowed uniforms, towels, tools, and staff badges. Your own equipment comes back with you. These gifts are yours to keep, and work outside the bathhouse.

## Collect Your Pay

Each participating adventurer, including Bobbin, chooses **one option** below. Duplicate choices are allowed. The number of additional consumable packages, if any, will be announced at checkout. A package is a complete **20-helper booklet, six-use bath kit, or eight-dose snackbox**; choose extras only from **1, 5, or 10**.

Recurring items require **no attunement**. An item recharges only after the rest specified in its description; passing it between people does not recharge it. Identical effects do not stack. Cosmetic effects grant no unlisted mechanical benefits. Record your chosen gift and any consumables on your character record once awarded.

## Choose Your Wages

### 1. Folded Friends — Paperwork With Benefits

**Booklet of 20 enchanted sheets; each is one use.** As an **action**, fold one into a paper bird, moth, or little person. It animates for **1 hour**, then becomes ordinary paper. You may have only **one** active at a time; animating another ends the first. The Tiny helper has **AC 13, 1 HP, walking speed 20 ft., flying speed 40 ft., and darkvision 60 ft.** It acts immediately after your turn and obeys your spoken instructions within **120 ft.**; beyond that distance it lands and waits.

It can scout, carry or fetch an unattended object weighing **5 lb. or less**, deliver a note, work a simple latch, or take the **Help action**, including distracting a creature within 5 ft. to help an attack. It cannot attack, activate magic items, pick locks, or perform skilled work independently. As an **action** within 120 ft., see and hear through it until your next turn starts; during that time you are blind and deaf to your own surroundings. Walls still block its senses, and it must physically reach a destination. At 0 HP the sheet tears and the use ends. No familiar bond or spell delivery. “Please do not feed the paperwork to Paa. He already knows too much.”

### 2. The Seven-Day Screen — Haunt-Vision

A **two-pound, portable black-and-white television** with rabbit-ear aerials, a cracked glass screen, and a tuning knob labeled **LIVING / DEAD / CUSTOMER SUPPORT**. It needs no electricity. The picture rolls, the speaker crackles and pops, and changing channels occasionally produces a damp handprint on the inside of the glass.

**Once per long rest, action:** tune to a point you can see within **60 ft.** A tiny, long-haired ghost crawls out of a flickering screen-shaped apparition there. She looks exactly like an image escaping an old black-and-white TV: monochrome, grainy, crossed by horizontal scan lines, and prone to skipping a frame. For **10 minutes, concentration**, the television shows what she sees and plays what she hears.

As a **bonus action**, move the projection up to **30 ft.**, keeping it within **500 ft.** of you. She crawls across floors, climbs through the air, and flattens into static to pass through openings at least an inch wide; solid barriers still block her. She is a visible, immaterial viewpoint and cannot interact with objects, attack, cross planes, or deliver spells. The feed uses your normal vision, including darkvision, with no additional senses. Her occasional crackles and pops are audible within **10 ft.**, so this is hardly discreet surveillance. After seven days the screen displays **YOUR FREE TRIAL HAS EXPIRED. YOU HAVE NOT. YET.** The item keeps working; the subscription threat is advertising.

### 3. Camera Obscurely

**One charge, regained when its user finishes a short or long rest. Every photograph costs this charge**, whether an ordinary keepsake or a revealing flash. As an **action**, expend a **1st-level or higher spell slot** to restore the charge for another photograph.

**Revealing flash — Action:** aim the camera in a direction of your choice and photograph a **30-foot cone originating from you**. Invisible creatures there, and creatures in the **corresponding space of the overlapping Border Ethereal**, make a **DC 17 Charisma save** unless behind total cover. On failure, a **ghostly outline** clings to the creature and is visible to everyone for **1 minute, or until this camera uses its revealing flash again**, whichever comes first. It suppresses invisibility's benefits for that duration; an ethereal creature remains on its own plane. On success, it stays hidden in the scene, **but its outline still appears in the photograph**. A visible extraplanar creature photographs normally; being from another plane grants no immunity. No concentration required. Starting a new revealing flash ends the previous flash's outlines before resolving the new saves.

The camera **immediately prints a permanent instant photograph** of the scene and any captured outlines. These show shape and position at that moment, not identity or a disguised creature's true form. Seeing an ethereal outline does not let you attack or interact across planes. No spirit trapping, damage, or access to distant nonoverlapping planes. Film is magically supplied but loses its magic when printed. “A Polter-oid. Shake it all you like; that ghost still blinked. Additional memories require additional spell slots.”

### 4. Polter-Guest Doorknob

**Once per short rest, action:** hold the brass knob against one closed door within reach. For **1 minute**, hear sounds and speech within **30 ft. on its other side** as though standing there; understand only languages you normally understand. You also sense whether the door is locked or magically warded, but learn neither the ward's workings nor how to bypass it. Does not open the door, identify speakers, detect silent creatures, or defeat effects that block divination. Magical doors work unless their protections block the effect. When the minute ends, a spectral voice audibly announces on both sides, “Thank you for allowing us to monitor your conversation for training purposes.” Excellent surveillance, appalling customer service.

### 5. Bath Kit of the Living Dead

**Six consumable bath bombs**, plus ordinary soap, a scrub mitt, a folding washbasin, and candles shaped like concerned skulls. Each bomb can be used in either way:

- **Emergency Spa — Action:** throw it up to **30 ft.** to create a **15-ft.-radius cloud** of cold scented mist, heavily obscured for **1 minute** or until dispersed by a strong wind. No damage; it does not extinguish magical fire. Works on dry ground.
- **Rest in Peace and Eucalyptus — Ten-minute soak:** dissolve it in a bath large enough for the bathers. Up to **six undead creatures**, each with at least 1 HP, spending the full ten minutes bathing regain **3d8 + their own proficiency bonus HP**. The salts stitch decayed flesh, polish bone, and can reattach an existing severed body part if it is present throughout the soak. They cannot create missing parts, increase maximum HP, animate a corpse, revive destroyed undead, or restore a creature to life. Each undead can benefit **once per long rest**. **Living creatures regain no HP**; they merely get clean, smell faintly of embalming herbs, and look fashionably pallid for an hour. The soak may occupy part of a short rest but does not shorten that rest.

Both modes leave you smelling of lavender and a recently opened tomb. **FOR UNDEAD SKIN. DEAD SERIOUS MOISTURIZING.** “For external use only. Please check all limbs against your receipt before leaving.”

### 6. Soot-Sprite Union Button

**Once per short rest, action:** pin it to your clothing and summon **three soot sprites for 1 hour**. You may have only one crew active; summoning another dismisses the previous crew. Each is Tiny, with **AC 13, 1 HP, speed 30 ft., and climb speed 30 ft.** They follow spoken instructions within **60 ft.** of you, acting together immediately after your turn. Each can carry **20 lb.**; all three can cooperate to move **60 lb.** They haul coal, fetch unattended objects, sweep, wash dishes, fold clothes, tie simple knots, and operate ordinary handles or levers. One of them invariably lies down beneath anything heavy and demands better working conditions.

After **10 uninterrupted minutes**, they can clean and dry up to **three objects**, each no larger than a backpack, and repair one tear/break up to **1 ft.** across in each, provided no material is missing. They can also take the **Help action for one physical tool check per round** when simple assistance actually helps; the whole crew counts as one helper. They cannot attack, assist attacks, scout independently, manipulate worn/held property without permission, activate magic items, or perform skilled work on their own. No restoring HP, charges, magic, or value; no curse removal or deliberate erasure of writing/evidence. A sprite reduced to 0 HP vanishes; replacements wait for the next activation. They expect star-shaped sugar candy. Apparently crumbs are “a management position.”

### 7. Final Girl Slippers — Sensible Shoes, Terrible Decisions

Plaid bath slippers embroidered with tiny emergency-exit arrows. Whenever someone suggests splitting up, they squeak **ABSOLUTELY FUCKING NOT**. This is fashion advice, not supernatural knowledge. Anyone can wear them; surviving the film is not a gender requirement.

**Once per short rest, reaction**, when a creature you can see targets you with an attack, **before the attack roll**: move up to **10 ft. without provoking opportunity attacks**. If you finish outside that attack's reach/range or behind total cover from the attacker, the triggering attack misses you; otherwise, resolve it normally from your new position. The attacker can continue its turn, including any remaining movement and attacks. This is movement, not teleportation: your speed must be above 0, normal movement restrictions apply, and you need an unoccupied, supported route. No walking through walls, breaking a grapple, or dodging an attack already rolled. They fit over ordinary footwear and grant no AC bonus. “Congratulations. For once, you did not investigate the noise.”

### 8. Grave Mistake Bathrobe

**Works only while wearing no armor; clothing and jewelry are fine.** The robe supplies no AC bonus. **Once per long rest, reaction** when you would take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage: **prevent all of those damage types from that single damage instance**, including magical weapon damage. Other damage types and nondamage effects still apply. For example, it stops the slash of a flaming sword but not its fire damage. Later attacks, subsequent damage ticks, and damage to anyone else are unaffected.

Before sleeping, you may set it to **Night Watch**. While you are asleep, it automatically spends that same use on the **first qualifying damage instance**, requiring no reaction; if the use is spent, it cannot protect you again until recharged. You can change the setting freely while awake. For one minute after either trigger you look like a damp, translucent ghost; cosmetic. The belt sighs like a disappointed parent. **DO NOT DISTURB. EVEN WITH AN AXE.**

### 9. Lake-Stone of Someone Waiting

**Once per short rest, action:** send one **silent vision lasting up to six seconds** to a creature you personally know on the **same plane**, at any distance. Choose an image or brief moving scene from your own memory, something you currently see, or your imagination: a familiar doorway, a candle in a window, someone waiting beside an empty chair. It carries **no sound, spoken words, or readable text**. The recipient feels a moment of warmth, knows who sent it, and recognizes it as a mental vision; it neither blocks their actual senses nor forces an emotion or action.

A willing recipient may immediately return **one silent vision of up to six seconds** under the same rules. An unwilling creature can ignore it; an asleep creature receives a comforting dream but cannot reply through this use. The stone shows only what someone deliberately chooses to send: it does not read minds, reveal anyone's location or health automatically, or verify whether an imagined scene is true. Without a reply, the sender gets no confirmation of delivery. Wards that block magical communication still apply. The stone resembles a tiny moon. “Show your mother you're eating properly. Perhaps omit the part where dinner was haunted.”

### 10. Skiving Snackbox — Medicinal Truancy

**Eight paired treats: two of each below.** Eat an orange half as a **bonus action** for its benefit and comic symptom. The matching brown half can be eaten as a **bonus action** to end the symptom without ending the benefit. Each pair is one consumable use; the brown half grants no separate benefit. Symptoms cause no actual disease, damage, loss of actions, or unconsciousness.

| Treat | Benefit | Entirely respectable excuse |
|---|---|---|
| **Puking Pastille** | End the poisoned condition on yourself; gain **advantage on saves against poison for 1 hour**. Does not neutralize an ongoing poison or its other effects. | For ten minutes, cough up harmless rainbow bubbles. “I cannot attend the meeting. I am producing management's diversity statement.” |
| **Fainting Fancy** | End **one charmed or frightened effect** on yourself. For **10 minutes**, gain advantage on Deception checks to pretend to be asleep, unconscious, or unwell; you must still act convincingly. No immunity to being charmed/frightened again. | For ten minutes, a tiny violin plays whenever you swoon. You remain conscious unless actually asleep or incapacitated. |
| **Nosebleed Nougat** | Gain **15 temporary HP for 1 hour**; temporary HP do not stack. | A harmless, convincing nosebleed lasts ten minutes. “My condition is extremely serious and suspiciously delicious.” |
| **Fever Fudge** | Gain **resistance to cold damage for 1 hour**. | For ten minutes, steam rises from your ears and your forehead feels feverish. Thermometers read **EXCUSED**. |
