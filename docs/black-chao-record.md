## Fiend Features

**Dark One's Blessing.** When Chao reduces an enemy to 0 HP, he gains **17 temporary HP** (Charisma modifier + Warlock level). He also gains this benefit when someone else reduces an enemy within 10 feet of him to 0 HP.

**Dark One's Own Luck.** When making an ability check or saving throw, add 1d10 after seeing the roll but before its effects occur. **Five uses per long rest**, no more than once per roll.

**Fiendish Resilience.** After a short or long rest, choose a damage type other than force. Chao has resistance to that type until he chooses another with this feature.

## Liáo Xi (燎息), “Scorching Breath”

*+1 transforming jian — martial melee weapon; versatile, finesse, transforming*

Liáo Xi grants **+1 to attack and damage rolls** in either form, already included in the attack table. Its sword deals 1d8 + 5 slashing with one hand or 1d10 + 5 slashing with two hands.

**Scorching Breath.** As a bonus action, speak the command word to ignite the blade. It sheds bright light for 40 feet and dim light for another 40 feet, and deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on every hit while ablaze. The flames end when Chao repeats the command word as a bonus action, drops the weapon, or sheathes it.

**Transforming.** As a bonus action, transform the weapon between sword and bow forms. The bow deals 1d8 + 5 piercing plus 1d6 fire damage. The existing campaign record also gives it **150/600-ft. range**, self-created burning arrows, and automatic ignition when transformed into bow form; these details are retained, although the shorter new sheet does not repeat them.
