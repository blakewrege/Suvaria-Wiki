🛠 The Gadget Grotto 🛠¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

"Where ingenuity meets insanity!"

Shop Description¶

Deep beneath the cobbled streets and rusted pipelines of the city—hidden behind a series of mechanical locks—lies The Gadget Grotto: a subterranean emporium of invention and chaos. Part workshop, part black-market tech hub, and part mad scientist’s playground, it’s now courting investments from the E.A.R.S. (Experimental Artifice & Research Syndicate).

This massive underground complex is lit by brass sconces, glowing blueprints, and crackling plasma coils. Cogs grind within the walls, and steampipes vent bursts of hissing vapor while mechanical arms scuttle overhead, adjusting precariously stacked shelves of half-finished contraptions, impossible blueprints, and clockwork creatures with questionable AI ethics.

🔐 Security Measures¶

  1. The JUDGE
  2. A sentient automaton guarding the entrance.
  3. Scans all visitors with its omniscient monocle eye.

  4. If someone fails its riddle-based authentication, they’re subjected to mildly humiliating punishments (e.g., gluing boots to the floor, floating for 5 minutes, or forced to speak in rhyme).

  5. Clockwork Swarm Sentinels

  6. Beetle-sized automatons patrolling shelves.

  7. Zap shoplifters with tiny electroshock pincers or inject mild sedatives for easy capture.

  8. Vault Access via DNA & Riddle Authentication

  9. A shifting metal puzzle box must be solved to open restricted schematics & weaponry.

  10. Anti-Duplication Field

  11. Prevents thieves from simply copying blueprints (except under incredibly convoluted circumstances).

🧑‍🔬 Meet Picky Pauly¶

“If it ain’t perfect, it ain’t good enough!”

  • The caretaker and salesman of The Gadget Grotto.
  • A small, wiry man with an ever-present look of scrutiny.
  • Inspects every gadget like it personally offended him, and analyzes customers as though they’re malfunctioning devices.
  • Picky Eater: “This soup is too symmetrical! It needs chaos!”

Despite his exacting standards, he’s brilliant at modifying, upgrading, and repairing gadgets. If a player earns his respect (e.g., by fixing a flaw in one of his designs), he may offer them rare schematics or custom-built tools.

🔑 Special Deals & Services¶

  • 🛠 Custom Modifications
    Pauly can enhance a gadget for an extra fee—but be ready for 20 minutes of him complaining first.

  • 💰 Mystery Box Special (150 gp)
    A random gadget from the “restricted vault.” Could be incredible or utterly useless.
