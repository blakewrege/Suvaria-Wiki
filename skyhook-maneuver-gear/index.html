✈️ Skyhook Maneuver Gear¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

Wondrous Item, Rare (requires attunement; can only be worn with Light Armor or no armor)

📝 Description:¶

This sleek, gnomish-designed harness is crafted from polished brass fittings, sturdy leather straps, and compact steam pistons. Twin grappling hooks rest snugly in side-mounted holsters, attached to durable, clockwork-reinforced cables.

⚙️ Features:¶

  • Vertical Grapple (Movement Action):
    You fire a grappling hook up to 60 feet toward a solid surface, large object, or creature. You instantly pull yourself to that point or swing freely.

  • Requires an Acrobatics or Athletics check (DC 13/8) to successfully maneuver.

  • Boost Jets (Bonus Action; 3/Day):
    Activate short-burst steam jets for an immediate 20-foot burst of speed.

  • As part of this movement, you may make one melee attack with advantage.

  • All three charges replenish after completing a long rest.

  • Limited Steam Fuel:
    After every 10 uses of Vertical Grapple, the Skyhook Gear must be recharged.

  • Spend 10 minutes refueling and maintaining the gear with basic supplies to restore its functionality.

🚨 Skyhook Mishap Table:¶

When you fail an Acrobatics or Athletics check using Vertical Grapple, roll a d6 to determine the malfunction:

d6 Mishap Result
1–3 Jammed: Gear jams and cannot be used again until the end of your next turn.
4–5 Misfire: You fall prone, but only lose the movement required to stand up again.
6 Tangled Cables: Your movement speed is reduced to zero until your turn ends as you untangle cables.

🎖️ Special Note for Fang Ro (Monk Character):¶

Given Fang Ro's exceptional agility and speed as a Monk, the Skyhook Maneuver Gear complements and amplifies your natural mobility, enabling dramatic aerial combat, swift repositioning, and daring escapes. This gear aligns seamlessly with your playstyle, allowing rapid access to high vantage points and agile engagement in battle.

  • Pratice makes perfect: After 3 days of dedicated practice, Fang Ro permanently reduces the Acrobatics DC for Vertical Grapple from 13 to 8.
