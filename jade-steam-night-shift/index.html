Jade Steam — Your First and Possibly Last Night Shift¶

Session 078 · Temporary Staff Welcome Packet

“Congratulations. You entered a spa and have progressed directly to unpaid training. The uniform is compulsory. Enthusiasm will be assessed separately.” — Madam Yu-Banya

What the Hell Is Going On?¶

After Deep Snoat's revelations and a round of questionable tattoo decisions, a bath sounded sensible. Then the tenth bell rang. The spa changed, the exit stopped being an exit, and your clothes were suddenly in another department.

What the staff have told you: Someone arranged your overnight invitation, but the name is missing. Madam Yu-Banya says the sleeping lotus carried you safely through the change of shifts. The mortal entrance returns at dawn. Until your guest registration is sorted out, temporary employment keeps the bathhouse from classifying you as something it can put on a shelf.

Gold is not accepted on the night shift. Service, promises, and certain personal intangibles are recognized currency. Your temporary staff agreement requires useful work until sunrise, with breaks. No blood signature or permanent surrender of your name is required. Read anything else before signing it.

People You Have Been Introduced To¶

Person What you know
Madam Yu-Banya The night manager: crimson robes, towering silver hair, golden combs, and the expression of someone who has billed a thunderstorm for damages. She handles your temporary employment and insists the anonymous invitation is highly irregular.
Mihiro A small Mian girl in a patched green work coat, oversized wooden sandals, and a red hair cord. She knows the service passages and has already demonstrated better survival instincts than management. She will accompany you, explain chores, and point out which instructions are meant literally.
Maa and Paa — Mina and Hiro Mihiro's parents, currently goats while she works off their family debt. Maa inspects laundry; Paa eats paperwork. Mihiro would very much like her parents back.
Nohbody A black-robed, white-masked spirit whose offers of gold come with far too much attention. Mihiro says he copies the desires around him and becomes bigger and hungrier when fed. She recommends kindness, firm boundaries, and keeping your dumplings.

Bobbin is with you, looking disproportionately qualified to operate the cleaning cart. Staff have asked him to stop referring to the night as “a hostile exfoliation.”

Collect Your Kit — Five Staff Members¶

Each of the five player characters receives the same uniform and one towel, then chooses a funny name tag, two utility items, and one cleaning weapon or casting tool below. Every kit also includes slippers, work gloves, a belt pouch, a red-thread focus badge, and a waterproof component sachet. Choices may repeat; the supply closet has duplicates. Bobbin and Mihiro have their own staff basics and do not take one of your five kits.

These are temporary bathhouse loans, with no attunement. Their magic works only inside Jade Steam; return them before leaving. Outside combat you can exchange issued equipment at the cart. Exchanging never restores spent charges, and each character can carry only the listed allotment of enchanted loan equipment at once.

Uniforms — Dress for the Job You Fear¶

Everyone wears the same jade-green wrap robe, waterproof cream apron, and rubber-duck slippers. The apron reads TEMPORARY STAFF — PERMANENT LIABILITY. It resizes for your body, wings, horns, or other inconveniently heroic anatomy; the sash stays securely tied. Your only fashion choice is a name tag. Pick one below or invent your own; the title grants no extra powers or qualifications.

Name tag Fine print

The Sorcerer of Suds BORN WITH IT. WASHED WITH IT.
The Warlock of Warsh MY PATRON SAYS DRY CLEAN ONLY.
The Paladin of Porcelain SMITE THE MITE.
The Cleric of Deep Cleaning BLESS THIS MESS. THEN REMOVE IT.

Uniform protection: AC 18. Do not add your Dexterity modifier or combine it with another base-AC calculation. The uniform cannot be further enchanted or upgraded. Separate AC bonuses from spells or features still apply normally. It counts as clothing, requires no armor proficiency, and does not prevent unarmored class features. “Management has already enchanted it against injury, embarrassment, and requests for a raise. Two out of three is acceptable.”

Casters — Your Magic Still Works¶

The red-thread badge and every issued casting tool can serve as a focus for your class's spells that use noncostly, unconsumed material components. The sachet supplies those mundane components too. A worn badge leaves your hands free, but you still need a free hand for somatic/material components unless a feature says otherwise. You may use the same hand holding a focus for a spell's material and somatic components as normal.

You keep your prepared/known spells, spell slots, class features, tattoos, and worn jewelry. The uniform grants no bonus to spell attacks or spell save DCs. Components with a listed gold cost, or components the spell consumes, must still come from your belongings. Staff can bring your own required components from your locker on request; this does not conjure free diamonds or replenish supplies. A wizard's spellbook can likewise be retrieved for preparation or rituals. Your spell's normal requirements still apply.

You can cast spells normally while carrying a cleaning implement. The tool attacks below are optional equipment powers, not extra spells, and do not automatically gain features that apply only to casting a spell or cantrip.

Your Emergency Towel¶

An enormous towel embroidered DON'T PANIC. IT ENCOURAGES THEM. Everyone receives one.

Once per short rest, choose one use:

  • Towel of Protection — Reaction: When you or a willing creature within 5 feet takes acid, cold, fire, necrotic, or psychic damage, reduce one instance of that damage by 2d8. You must be able to reach the creature. Multiple towels cannot reduce the same damage instance.
  • Please Hold Still — Action: End blindness on yourself or a willing creature within 5 feet caused specifically by nonmagical slime, soot, soap, or another removable surface coating. This does not cure disease, injury, magical blindness, or a spell.

After use, it remains a perfectly good ordinary towel. Its magic recharges when its assigned wearer finishes a short or long rest; swapping towels does not grant extra uses. It also dries you, preserves your dignity, and silently judges your folding.

Utility Cupboard — Choose Two¶

Unless stated otherwise, each use takes an action and requires a free hand. These tools help solve physical problems; they do not automatically remove a curse or replace good judgment.

Utility What it does
Sponge of Unreasonable Absorbency Touch an unattended pool/spill to absorb up to 1 cubic foot of nonmagical liquid; that is also its capacity. Squeeze it into a container to empty it. Cannot pull liquid from a creature, remove bodily fluids, absorb a magical effect, or turn stored liquid into a damaging spray. Wear the gloves for hazardous spills.
Polter-Tongs Manipulate one unattended object of 10 lb. or less within 10 ft. that you can see, moving it up to 10 ft. Uses your hand's motion; cannot attack, pick locks, steal worn/held items, pass through barriers, or perform skilled work independently. Useful for suspicious teeth.
Rubber Duck of Due Diligence Set it in liquid. For 1 minute it paddles at up to 10 ft. per round along the surface flow, quacking at visible obstructions. It cannot sense poison, magic, creatures, depth, or hidden passages. It has very strong opinions about drains but no special knowledge of them.
OSHA-Approved Soap Three doses for the night. Coat one 5-ft. square within reach on solid ground for 1 minute, until washed away, or until someone wipes it up with an action. The first creature entering it on a turn makes a DC 15 Dexterity save or falls prone; at most once per creature per turn. It affects allies too and does not stack with itself.

The jar tolerates ordinary corrosive cleaning chemicals; it is not immune to attacks or supernatural substances. No stasis, spirit imprisonment, or extradimensional storage. Label reads IF IT HAS A LAWYER, ASK FIRST.
Squeegee of Clear Intentions Wipe up to 5 ft. square of reachable glass, tile, or metal clean of nonmagical surface condensation, grease, and grime. Does not erase writing, dissolve an object, or dispel a magical effect. It squeaks like a very disappointed violin.
Inspector's Bubble Lens Once per short rest, after rolling an Investigation check to examine a visible physical mess, mechanism, or evidence, but before the result is declared, add 1d4. Gloves of Absolutely Not Elbow-length work gloves. Handle filthy or ordinarily caustic objects safely when no damage is otherwise called for. Once per short rest, reaction: reduce acid damage to yourself by 1d8 + PB. Does not stack with an Emergency Towel against the same damage. The gloves visibly recoil before you touch anything especially disgusting.

Cleaning Weapons and Casting Tools — Choose One¶

Choose Strength, Dexterity, or one of your spellcasting abilities for an issued tool's attacks and save DC; use that same ability until you finish a short rest. At level 12, proficiency bonus is normally +4.

Tool attack bonus = proficiency bonus + chosen ability modifier. Tool save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + chosen ability modifier. These are separate from your normal spell figures. All tools are magical; you are proficient with them. There is no hidden +1/+2 bonus.

Tool Combat rules Household application
Mop of Seven Ends Two-handed melee weapon, 10-ft. reach, 2d8 + modifier force. Once per turn on hit, push a Huge or smaller target 5 ft., or clear one 5-ft. patch of slime in its space. Works with Extra Attack. Wringing the heads makes seven distinct noises of disgust.
Sacred Plunger of Last Resort Two-handed melee weapon, 5-ft. reach, 2d10 + modifier bludgeoning. Critical on 19–20 against constructs or animated plumbing. Works with Extra Attack and features requiring a melee weapon. The handle reads IN CASE OF APOCALYPSE, BREAK SEAL.
Ghost-Hose Nozzle Two-handed ranged weapon, range 60/120 ft., 2d10 + modifier radiant. Once per turn on hit, reduce a Huge or smaller target's speed by 10 ft. until your next turn starts; multiple hoses do not stack this reduction. Works with Extra Attack. Self-contained reservoir; no hose tether required. WHO YOU GONNA SOAP?
Sponge Knuckles One-handed melee weapon, 5-ft. reach, 2d6 + modifier force. Works with Extra Attack. After a hit, you may harmlessly clean one nonmagical surface smear from the target; this does not remove conditions or defenses. Requires a hand to wield; not an unarmed strike or free-hand focus. Each punch makes a magnificent squeak.
Hex & Shoulders Atomizer One-handed focus. Action: make one ranged spell attack using your tool attack bonus, range 60 ft., dealing 3d8 + modifier radiant on a hit. Does not use Extra Attack or spend a spell slot. Removes flakes. Occasionally existential ones.
Dustbuster of the Damned Two-handed focus. Action: one creature you see within 30 ft. makes a Strength save against your tool DC. Failure: 4d8 force and pulled 10 ft. toward you if Huge or smaller; success: half damage, no pull. Does not capture or contain the creature. The filter bag is marked SOULS SOLD SEPARATELY.
Bell-on-a-Stick One-handed focus. Action: one creature you see within 60 ft. makes a Constitution save against your tool DC: 3d8 thunder, half on success. Alternatively, ring to outline invisible creatures within 15 ft. until your next turn starts, removing invisibility's benefits; blocked by total cover. Alerts housekeeping, ghosts, and everyone trying to nap.
Salt Scattergun Two-handed tool, three charges for the night. Action: 15-ft. cone, Dexterity save against your tool DC: 4d8 radiant, half on success. On failure, a target cannot benefit from invisibility until the end of its next turn. Please season responsibly.

For an action-based tool power, take that action instead of casting a spell or using the Attack action; Extra Attack does not multiply it. Bonus actions and reactions remain subject to your normal features. Trading or replacing a charged tool never refills it: each character receives only one three-charge allotment for each charged tool type over the night.

S.C.R.U.B. — Your Traveling Cleaning Cart¶

Sanitation Cart for Regrettably Unclothed Bathers. A brass trolley with six folding legs, mop racks, sample drawers, and a bell that prints workplace-incident slips. It follows the party whenever the route permits. Bobbin usually steers; Mihiro knows where to park it.

It carries spare mundane towels, buckets, soap, chalk, salt, gloves, tongs, sealing paper, a 50-ft. rope, basic cleaning supplies, and component sachets. These are supplies, not unlimited alchemical ammunition or precious spell components. Item exchanges take a minute outside combat, and supplies cannot recharge powers already used.

Between encounters it walks at 30 ft. speed, climbs ordinary stairs, and folds to fit a 3-ft.-wide passage. It cannot fly, teleport, open locked doors, swim safely through deep water, scout independently, or know a route nobody has shown it. If the party takes an inaccessible shortcut, leave it parked and carry the needed equipment; it can rejoin by a known accessible route.

In combat, a character within 5 ft. may use a bonus action to direct it up to 30 ft. along a clear floor route. The cart can move this way only once per round, has no initiative or attacks, and cannot ram creatures. Taking one accessible mundane item uses your normal object interaction. It is a Medium object, AC 15, 40 HP; at 0 HP its legs lock instead of exploding. Ten minutes of mundane repair restores it to working order with full HP. Repair requires a safe pause and cannot be repeated as combat healing. It does not grant automatic cover; use the actual positioning and ordinary cover rules.

The Work Board¶

Choose where to start. Mihiro accompanies you and Bobbin brings the cart. You are not required to finish all five jobs before an approved break or emergency reassignment.

Card Assignment Management's helpful advice
A — Linen Annex Seven Count and fold forty-nine robes; isolate the extra one. “If it folds you first, ring for assistance.”
B — Honeymoon Suite / The Exorcise Room Clean the departing guests' spectacular mess and prepare the suite for another booking. “Start at the ceiling. You will understand why.”
C — Long-Distance Guest Care Help bathe a mountain-lake patron who traveled an extraordinary distance for treatment. “Courteous. Gentle. Upwind.”
D — Boiler-Crew Supper Deliver meals and feed the smallest workers as well as their supervisor. “Soot sprites are staff, not fuel.”
E — Lost-Property Audit Check the cedar lockers and stop the paper birds rearranging ownership. Your belongings are stored there. “A bird carrying a receipt is still a bird.”

Rules Worth Remembering¶

  1. Keep your red thread on and do useful work. Ask before taking a break. Honest mistakes and choosing a different job are not refusal of duty.
  2. Do not accept Nohbody's gold or feed his growing appetite. You may be kind without making a sale.
  3. Read the label before opening a valve, eating a snack, or promising anything in perpetuity.
  4. Your usual abilities still matter. A clever spell or practical solution can solve a chore; you do not need to invent a dice roll for everything.
  5. The cart has a wet-floor sign. Consider using it before the floor develops a legal defense.

Questions about survival: Mihiro. Questions about invoices: Madam Yu-Banya. Questions about the smell: please stop asking.
