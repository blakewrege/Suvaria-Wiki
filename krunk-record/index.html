Krunk “the Drunk” — Campaign Ledger¶

Player-facing campaign record. Level-12 sheet and item cards, updated with player-confirmed corrections.

Half-Orc Paladin 12 — Oath of Vengeance
Background: Soldier (Officer) • Alignment: Neutral Good • Holy Symbol: The White Hand
AC: 20 / 21 with a shield • HP Maximum: 107 • Speed: 30 ft • Initiative: +0
Proficiency Bonus: +4 • Spell Save DC: 16, or 18 with Excalibur • Spell Attack: +8, or +10 with Excalibur
Hit Dice: 12d10 • Passive Perception: 11

Krunk has Extra Attack, so he attacks twice whenever he takes the Attack action.

Weapon Group Specialization — Clubs¶

Krunk uses Club Specialization:

  • When Krunk critically strikes with a club-group weapon, the target is knocked prone.
  • Whenever Krunk makes a Strength (Athletics) check to shove a target, he deals damage equal to his Strength modifier (4) whether the shove succeeds or fails.

Core Features¶

  • Darkvision. Krunk can see in darkness out to 60 feet.
  • Menacing. Krunk is proficient in Intimidation.
  • Relentless Endurance. Once per long rest, when reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, Krunk drops to 1 HP instead.
  • Savage Attacks. On a critical hit with a melee weapon, roll one additional weapon damage die.
  • Resilient (Constitution). Krunk is proficient in Constitution saving throws.
  • Great Weapon Fighting. When Krunk rolls a 1 or 2 on a damage die for a two-handed or versatile melee weapon wielded with two hands, he may reroll that die and must use the new result.
  • Divine Health. Krunk is immune to disease.
  • Lay on Hands. Krunk has a pool of 60 HP per long rest. He may also spend 5 HP from the pool to cure one disease or neutralize one poison.
  • Divine Sense — 5/long rest. As an action, Krunk detects celestials, fiends, and undead within 60 feet that are not behind total cover.
  • Divine Smite. After hitting with a melee weapon, expend a spell slot for 2d8 radiant damage, plus 1d8 per slot level above 1st to a maximum of 5d8, and another 1d8 against a fiend or undead.
  • Improved Divine Smite. Krunk's melee weapon hits deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage.
  • Aura of Protection. Krunk and friendly creatures within 10 feet add his +4 Charisma modifier to saving throws while he is conscious.
  • Aura of Courage. Krunk and friendly creatures within 10 feet cannot be frightened while he is conscious.
  • Relentless Avenger. After hitting with an opportunity attack, Krunk may move up to half his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

Channel Divinity¶

  • Abjure Enemy. As an action, choose one creature within 60 feet. It makes a Wisdom save; fiends and undead have disadvantage. On a failure it is frightened for 1 minute and its speed becomes 0. On a success its speed is halved for 1 minute or until it takes damage.
  • Vow of Enmity. As a bonus action, choose a creature within 10 feet. Krunk has advantage on attack rolls against it for 1 minute or until it drops to 0 HP or falls unconscious.

Roleplaying Notes¶

  • Traits: Fight the greater evil. Haunted by war and nightmares. His homeland was ravaged by war.
  • Ideal: Independence. No mercy for the wicked.
  • Bond: “I fight for the helpless by any means necessary.”
  • Flaw: “My hatred is blind and unwavering.” Retribution comes before restraint.

Current Important Equipment¶

Current attunement: Excalibur and Maul of Maelstroms (offense), plus Veil of Shadows (defense). Ash's Righteous Chainsaw Blade is owned but not attuned; its listed magical properties are an item reference, not active attuned benefits.

Ash's Righteous Chainsaw Blade +2¶

Rare, two-handed; requires attunement by a paladin — not currently attuned

  • Grants +2 to attack and damage rolls.
  • Deals 2d6 slashing damage before modifiers.
  • Objects and creatures made of wood are vulnerable to damage from the weapon.
  • On a critical hit against an undead creature, it deals an extra 2d8 fire damage.
  • Chainsaw Roar. At the start of combat, Krunk may activate the roar. Everyone within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution save or be deafened until the end of their next turn.

Maul of Maelstroms +2¶

Uncommon, two-handed; requires attunement

  • Grants +2 to attack and damage rolls. The +2 damage bonus is lightning damage.
  • Battle Cry. Once per long rest, Krunk may shout and slam an enemy for an extra 1d10 thunder damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength save or be pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.
  • The weapon gives a slight shock when picked up and seems to vibrate with energy.

Historian's Horologe¶

Uncommon; does not require attunement

This magical pocket watch allows its owner to teleport back to a previously set position once per day.

Veil of Shadows — Shield of the Void¶

Rare, requires attunement — currently attuned

A large black amulet. Once per day, Krunk may use a bonus action or reaction and speak its command word—“FUCK NO!”—to create a lightly obscuring 10-foot aura. Until the start of his next turn, everything in the aura has resistance to all damage.

Excalibur, the Promised Dawn¶

Krunk currently carries and is attuned to Excalibur, the Promised Dawn, a +2 versatile longsword. With Strength +4, he attacks at +10 and deals 1d8 + 6 slashing one-handed or 1d10 + 6 slashing two-handed. Once per turn against a creature Excalibur deems evil, Radiant Edge adds +4 radiant damage.

Excalibur's Spellcasting Enhancement: Krunk's spell save DC increases from 16 to 18 and his spell attack modifier increases from +8 to +10 while he wields Excalibur or keeps a hand on its hilt while it is sheathed, provided he is not wielding another weapon. A shield is allowed. Releasing the hilt or drawing another weapon ends the bonuses.
