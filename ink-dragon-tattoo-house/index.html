Ink Dragon Tattoo House¶

Pain is included. Regret is billed separately.

Consultation: 10 gp, credited toward any commissioned tattoo. Every symbol is translated before application. Every magical effect—and every known drawback—is written down before payment. No permanent magical work is performed on an intoxicated client, regardless of how persuasive his emergency sake may be.

Prices are negotiable. Zhen accepts reasonable offers, useful barter, rare pigments, documented field testing, and interest-free installments. She will reduce the price before she reduces the safety.

Art Ink — 10 to 500 gp¶

Work Price
Small linework, name, symbol, or uncomplicated mistake 10–50 gp
Full-color crest, spirit animal, memorial, or scar-weaving design 50–250 gp
Moonlit ink, slowly moving linework, harmless glow, or another cosmetic enchantment 150–500 gp

Art Ink carries no combat power and does not count against magical-ink capacity. Cover-ups, removals, and work around old scars are quoted after inspection.

Flash Ink lasts for its stated duration or until its single use is spent. It arrives charged, does not consume the wearer's magic, and does not recharge. A wearer can safely carry active Flash Ink designs equal to their proficiency bonus.

Dragon Sneeze¶

As an action, scratch one of the dragon's four seasoning runes. The ink dragon races up your body, braces its tiny claws across your ribs, and magically overinflates your lungs. You then unleash a sneeze in either a 15-foot cone or a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line, chosen when you activate it.

Dragon Sneeze save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Constitution modifier. Choose Strength or Constitution when the tattoo is applied. The dragon supplies the element; the wearer supplies either violent respiratory commitment or lungs built like siege equipment.

Each creature in the chosen area makes the rune's listed saving throw. On a failure, it takes 6d8 damage and suffers the listed rider. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no rider.

Ember Chili Dexterity 6d8 fire The creature catches fire for up to 1 minute. At the start of each of its turns, it takes 1d8 fire damage. The burning ends when a creature uses an action to extinguish it, when it is thoroughly doused, or when magic extinguishes the flames. Unattended flammable objects in the chosen area ignite.
Wintermint Constitution 6d8 cold Ice locks the creature in place, reducing its speed to 0 until the end of its next turn. On that turn, it may use an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against the Dragon Sneeze DC, ending the effect early on a success.

The sneeze instantly disperses mundane smoke, fog, and gas in the chosen area and flings unsecured Tiny objects 1d4 × 10 feet away without dealing damage. It is audible from 300 feet away. Everyone caught in it smells like the chosen seasoning for 1 hour, including creatures that succeeded on the save; Zhen insists this is how you know it worked.

Soulbound Ink — Permanent Magic¶

Soulbound Ink is custom work costing 125 to 10,000 gp. Minor techniques work at will or recharge after a short rest. Strong effects recharge after a long rest and also consume a spell slot, Hit Die, class resource, or another source of power agreed upon during consultation.

Magical-Ink Rules¶

  • A wearer may safely maintain permanent magical tattoos equal to their proficiency bonus.
  • A wearer may also carry active Flash Ink designs equal to their proficiency bonus. Extra Flash Ink remains inert until another design is spent or fades.
  • Only one magical tattoo may occupy each arm, leg, torso side, back, or head and neck.
  • Multiple copies of the same magical design do not grant additional uses or stack their effects.
  • Temporary ink does not count against capacity but cannot overlap another active magical design.
  • Magical tattoos do not increase Armor Class. If you want armor, Zhen knows several people who sell armor.
  • Ordinary injuries do not harm magical ink. Catastrophic wounds, dropping to 0 hit points from damage across the design, or deliberate mutilation may cause it to fade or go dormant.
  • Dormant or damaged tattoos can usually be repaired. Complete destruction of the tattooed skin may destroy the magic.
