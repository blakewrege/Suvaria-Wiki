Staff of Whispers of the Dark Lord¶

Archive audit notice: This handout was released before Session 077 and may contain older mechanics, names, links, or lore phrasing that still needs review. The table's latest ruling is authoritative.

Wondrous item (staff), legendary (requires attunement by a spellcaster)

Appearance¶

A gnarled blackwood shaft crowned by a dull, smoky crystal that seems to swallow light. Soft, sibilant whispers drift from its runes when you hold it. Faint spectral tendrils coil around the shaft, pulsing with each hushed word.

Lore¶

Forged deep beneath the dark spires of a forgotten fortress, the Staff of Whispers of the Dark Lord was born of both necromancy and treachery. Crafted by a sorcerer whose name was struck from history, the staff served as a vessel for imprisoning the soul of Gríma Wormtongue—a servant whose voice once toppled kings without ever raising a blade. Twisting in death as he did in life, Wormtongue’s spirit became bound to the staff’s smoky crystal, doomed to forever echo his master's will. Wherever the staff goes, so too does the breath of the Dark Lord, for it carries his voice to any who dare to listen.. Bound by foul magic, Wormtongue’s essence now writhes within the crystal, forever whispering Sauron’s will to any who dare wield it.

Sentience¶

  • Intelligence 12 | Wisdom 14 | Charisma 16
  • Alignment: Lawful Evil
  • Personality: Duplicitous and sycophantic, its voice slithers into the wielder’s mind, promising power in return for loyalty to Sauron.
  • Communication: Telepathic whispers in Common or Undercommon, audible only to the wielder.

Properties while Attuned¶

  • +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving-throw DCs of your spells.
  • Umbral Rift (Recharge 6): As a bonus action, you may meld into a shadow or dim light, becoming invisible and intangible, then reappear from another shadow or dim light within 60 ft. As part of this movement, you leave behind a silent shadowy illusion of yourself at your point of origin. This decoy lasts until the start of your next turn. Creatures attempting to target you must succeed on a Wisdom (Perception) check against your spell save DC. On a failure, they mistakenly target the decoy, which vanishes harmlessly.
  • Cloak of Flickering Gloom: While in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

Spell Usage (1/day each, cast at base level)¶

You may cast each of the following spells once per day using your spellcasting modifier. Use the checkboxes to track usage. All extra spells cast using the Staff of Whispers require a somatic component, even if the spell normally does not.

Rewards for Obedience¶

The staff hungers not only for loyalty, but for proof. If you commit vile acts at its urging—such as betraying your allies, defiling sacred sites, or murdering innocents—the staff rewards you by unlocking deeper powers. These spells are granted permanently once the required number of major evil deeds are completed. Use at your own peril.

Deed-Based Spell Unlocks¶

  • After 1st Major Deed
    Shadow of Moil, Compulsion (cast at base level, 1/day each)

  • After 2nd Major Deed
    Synaptic Static, Geas (cast at base level, 1/day each)

  • After 3rd Major Deed
    Otto’s Irresistible Dance, Mass Suggestion (cast at base level, 1/day each)

These spells become available only if the staff deems your actions sufficiently evil. The DM determines if an act qualifies. The staff’s whispers may tempt, but the choices—and consequences—are yours. (May cause alignment shift and deity changes)

Bond with the Dark Lord¶

Sauron is always aware of the staff’s general location (within a mile) and its “mood” (calm, restless, hungry) unless the staff is warded by nondetection or similar divination-blocking magic. Through this bond, the staff can convey simple concepts back to Sauron—alerts, questions, or brief commands—as if feeling the pulse of its own malevolent will.

Curse of Wormtongue’s Bond¶

Attunement to the staff begins the moment a creature touches it. Upon first contact, the staff whispers directly into the mind of the holder, inviting them to claim its power. If the creature fails a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw the attunement process completes instantly, binding the two together. The staff can only be attuned to one creature at a time. It may choose to end its attunement to the current wielder at any time, removing all ill effects—though the former wielder is left with a vague, hollow desire to steal it back.

  • Dark Obsession: At the end of each long rest, you must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or become obsessed until your next long rest ends. While obsessed:
  • You will not allow anyone to attempt to destroy the staff.
  • While weilding the staff you must use both hands.
  • You cannot willingly let the staff leave your possession; if separated, you automatically use your movement on your next turn to retrieve it.
  • Ally Protection: When you attempt to harm (including status effects) an ally or creature the staff “protects,” you must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the attack or harmful spell fizzles.
  • If you fail three DC 15 Wisdom or Charisma saves against the staff's whispers in a single day, the staff’s intelligence silently casts suggestion on you (self‑only, 1‑hour duration) with no saving throw to resist. (can be ended early with Dispell Magic or Remove Curse)

> Wormtongue’s ghostly voice never lets you rest.¶

Breaking the Curse¶

Only greater restoration, or wish can sever Wormtongue’s hold and end your attunement. The wielder cannot willingly break or end attunement on their own; another creature must perform the curse-breaking magic. Destroying the staff (mithral forge under a waning moon) releases a final scream: a 60‑ft radius of haunting whispers forces each creature there to make a DC 24 Wisdom save or be stunned for 1 hour.
