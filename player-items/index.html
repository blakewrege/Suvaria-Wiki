Equipment & Abilities¶

The master index for player-owned equipment, permanent upgrades, companion statistics, and other released character mechanics.

How this is organized: substantial items receive standalone handouts. Short legacy entries remain together in one catalog, while recent purchases live in each owner’s campaign ledger. This avoids creating fifty tiny pages without hiding anything.
